The narrow contribution

A promise-to-reality ledger,
not another incident report.

Reporting and debrief products already exist. Plan Kept focuses on whether an approved support was understood, available, offered, usable, and experienced.

02

Conflict without accusation

Different accounts become targeted clarification—not a credibility or danger score.

03

Repair with a return path

Named actions are not considered complete until the student can say whether support appeared in practice.
