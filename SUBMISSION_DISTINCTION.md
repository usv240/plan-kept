# Cross-submission distinction

Plan Kept is the Collaborative Partner entry. Its primary object is an existing support promise and its work is separate perspective gathering, controlled sharing, conflict clarification, human-approved repair and lived-experience follow-up. Its consequential boundary is participant privacy and human interpretation.

It does not share ColdClock's medicine logistics or clinical review. It does not share One Advisory's critical-facility incident fleet or managed resource-conflict architecture. Reused wake, verification, quarantine and observability primitives are disclosed infrastructure; the collaboration workflow, schema, UI, evaluation and submission narrative are independent.
