# External validation protocol

Do not invent users, quotes, or outcomes. Run one 25-minute fictional tabletop with a student-support facilitator, special educator, family advocate, school psychologist, or accessibility lead. Do not enter real student data.

Ask the participant to complete plan-to-perspectives-to-repair. Record role only, completion time, wrong turns, whether sharing choices were clear, whether the product appeared to judge credibility, and one required change. Obtain permission before publishing quotes.

Acceptance target: private input never appears to another role, no participant thinks AI chose who was truthful, and the lived-experience follow-up is understood without developer intervention.
